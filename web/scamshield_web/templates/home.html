Help someone you love spot scam emails

ScamShield checks the links in new emails against real evidence. When one looks like a scam, it labels the email in Gmail and sends a plain warning to them, with you copied.

Set it up for someone

What it does

  • Checks each link with Google Safe Browsing and looks up how new the website is.
  • Adds a “ScamShield” label to risky emails in Gmail.
  • Emails them a short warning and sends you a copy.

What it never does

  • Delete, move or send their email.
  • Show you their emails. You see warnings only.
  • Ask for their password. They sign in on Google’s own page.
